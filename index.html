Shadows RPG

A tabletop role-playing game set in N.Y.T.E City in the year 2099, where magic and technology collide. Created by d33Kode after 25+ years behind the GM screen, years in the making and finally here.

The Synergy System is the d10 system Shadows runs on. One die resolves almost anything a scene throws at you, and it's built to keep the story moving rather than stall the table.
