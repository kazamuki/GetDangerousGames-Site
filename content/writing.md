### Alabas Warbold No. 1
A young boy gifted with the power to see the spirit world is brought into the horrors of the gift. Haunted by nightmares only to find himself in another one. Facing death changes you. This becomes true for Alabas harshly as he confronts a monstrosity of the Umbralands.

### Once Bitten
A team returning to their hidden HQ is surprised to find intruders waiting for them. They don't know how it's possible the security systems were breached, but they have to deal with it. Except, there's something waiting for them they never could have anticipated.
→ https://belletristica.com/en/books/52354-once-bitten/chapter/294604-hunter-log-archival-index-27-3a
