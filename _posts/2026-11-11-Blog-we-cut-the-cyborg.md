**Two days after playtesting the Cyborg archetype, we cut it.** Deighton opened our September 26 session with an announcement he told us to buckle up for. After researching how other games handle cybernetics, he proposed removing Cyborg from Shadows as an archetype altogether.

**The reason comes down to one question** we'd been circling for weeks: what does a Cyborg get that a Professional who buys cybernetics doesn't? The honest answer turned out to be "not enough to build an archetype around." So cybernetics are becoming what they arguably always were: the way humans keep up with the supernatural, available to any mortal who can pay for them.

**Most of the work didn't go anywhere.** The mechanics we built over the past week, including a way to push your chrome past its limits and a nasty set of consequences when you push too far, are moving into the new cybernetics rules. The "I want to play the chrome guy" fantasy is getting a new home, too.

Shadows 1.0 now has four archetypes, and the cyborg isn't gone. It's everywhere.

The full story, including what got cut, what survived, and the new word for people who've gone past human, is on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/d33kode/posts/archetype-that-174694325).
