What can this archetype *not* do? What does failure look like for them, and is that failure actually theirs? Five questions, asked of all five Shadows archetypes, meant to catch the thing every game like this risks: an archetype that's really just another archetype wearing a different coat.

The answers got surprisingly personal — a shark that sets off danger sense before it's hungry, a werewolf whose real enemy is a broken schedule, a cyborg who might not have edges anymore at all.
