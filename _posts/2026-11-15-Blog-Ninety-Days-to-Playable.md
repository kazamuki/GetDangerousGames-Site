**We just set the clock.** At our October 1 meeting the team agreed on a rule for the next three months: finish what we have before building anything new. Every idea still gets a hearing, but the new ones go in a parking lot unless the game can't ship without them. The test is a single question, and it changed how fast the meeting moved.

**We also wrote down how four people decide things.** Some changes are big enough that everyone should see them in writing first, some just need a heads-up, and some you simply go do. It sounds like paperwork. It's the opposite.

**And Sanity finally has rules.** It's been on every character sheet since the beginning, and that day we argued over how long it takes to recover from watching something impossible walk through a wall. The answer wasn't an hour.

The meeting ended on a question nobody had planned for: how do you get better at something without spending a single point?
