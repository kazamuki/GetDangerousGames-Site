Alongside that, a few smaller rules questions got raised (headshot crits, a new GM term for time skips, Advantage/Disadvantage math) — not every week is a big reveal. Full notes on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/d33kode/posts/mapping-city-and-170113664).
