**The first real playtest of Shadows has a date: the week of December 7.** We're going to play it by the book, on purpose, with the one person who won't quietly fix our mistakes sitting in the GM chair.

**The Quick Start is taking shape.** A short rules primer for people who have never touched our five-hundred-page rulebook, plus a one-night adventure that starts in a diner, with cold coffee, a stamp on your hand and a missing day. Building it already turned up a few cracks we'd stopped being able to see.

**One of those cracks is a name.** Two stats in the game look alike on the page, and a new reader walks right into the confusion. We have a fix in mind that involves tiny icons, and a surprising amount of opinion about it.

**And we said yes to something one of us didn't want to say yes to.** Want to know what, and how the GM tools are coming along?
