Well, hello there! It has been a while since we posted.

There's a reason for that — namely, it wasn't working for us. We tried posting once a day, but it just became an advertising space for our daily video uploads to the YouTube channel.

I think these posts should start up again, but they'll only go out when something of note happens in the Get Dangerous community. That's why we're here today!

We've been experimenting with upgrading our YouTube content to be more than just clips of a VOD. As of the last week, we've started adding not just a title card to our videos, but a custom introduction for each one. I expect that once we get to the point of editing the videos down a bit more, the daily cadence will slow dramatically.

Speaking of daily uploads — that'll continue until at least the end of November. I had a personal goal of doing a daily upload for an entire year, and I'm already too far in to stop now! Thinking on it, we'll likely do a retrospective once that date hits and talk about what we're doing next and how. It's been fun learning some tricks to get these videos out quickly and always on time.

Hope you lovely people out there are doing well. We'll keep trying to make something of Get Dangerous and bring you updates when we feel like they'll matter!

— Get Dangerous Crew
