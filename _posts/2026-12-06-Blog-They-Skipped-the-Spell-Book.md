**Deighton's players took one look at our spell list and decided to make up their own.** The written spells felt too familiar, so they went straight to improvising. We spent most of the night working out what that tells us, and how much of it the rules already answer.

**Scott brought a rule he'd been using for twenty years without noticing it wasn't actually a rule.** It's about what everyone else does while one person makes the roll that matters. Deighton's reply was "yes, and," and the idea got bigger.

**We also found another rule that had only ever existed in Deighton's head.** Scott's reaction: "First time I've heard that."

**Plus:** a mage who spent five hours drawing on a bathroom mirror, why a cantrip is supposed to be weak, and the five characters heading to the table on December 7.
