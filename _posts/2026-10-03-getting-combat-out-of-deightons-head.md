Scott showed up to this meeting with a full list of combat questions he'd hit while actually writing the section — dodge limits, dice explosions, firing modes, range bands. We worked through all of it.

Highlight: what a die explosion on an attack actually does mechanically got Deighton genuinely excited once we landed on it. And range bands (Close/Short/Medium/Long/Extreme) turned out to be the cleanest design win of the year, according to D himself.

Full rundown of the debate — including where Ken and D disagreed on single-shot weapons — on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/d33kode/posts/getting-combat-170113040).
