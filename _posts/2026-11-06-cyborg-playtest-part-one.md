**At our September 24 meeting, we stopped talking about the Cyborg and started building one.** Deighton spent the week before putting together a full cybernetics system for Shadows: limb replacements, mods, software features, and the Neurocybernetic Interpreter, the AI layer that ties a cyborg's body to their machine parts. Ken, Scott, and Bill each built a cyborg from scratch to see whether the system holds up in practice.

**The builds were a blast.** One was a beam-cannon gunmaster with rocket-fast legs. One was a chrome-armed gunslinger hitting twice a turn. The third was an ex-corporate hunter whose cybernetic eyes and ears work together as a surveillance suite. Along the way, we found some rules that only ever existed in someone's head, and one dice mechanic that might not survive the month.

**Then the playtest showed us the real problem.** Once the characters were done, the team agreed that the Cyborg as currently written has too many moving parts. That led to a bigger question: when you pick Cyborg, what are you actually choosing that no one else gets? The answer we landed on is going to reshape the archetype, and Deighton came away with a clear target for the rebuild.

We also got a look at the latest digital character sheet updates, and they're good.
