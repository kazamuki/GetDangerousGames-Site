**Deighton came back from his table with a verdict.** The ruling we'd been holding since October finally got tested by real players, and the werewolf player had a great time. That answered one question and raised the next one right away.

**Now we're arguing about how hard a claw should hit.** Scott did the math, Deighton questioned the premise, and neither gave in. So for the next few weeks, the werewolves at Deighton's table are running two different rule sets to settle it.

**We also found a rule that was quietly ruining turns.** Players were paying for supernatural powers and then getting stuck with nothing else they could do. Once Deighton described it, the fix took about a minute.

**Plus:** what Luck does when you're trying to keep your mind intact, a piece of cyberware that outdoes the werewolf, and Deighton's thumbs-up on two things we told you about last time.
