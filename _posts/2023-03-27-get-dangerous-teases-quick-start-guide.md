Greetings!

The Get Dangerous Crew recently gathered to delve into the Shadows RPG Quick Start Guide. Our DM, d33kode, was unavailable, so we took matters into our own hands and made the most of our time together. [Watch the recap here](https://youtu.be/jog5a0UVmN0).

The Shadows RPG Quick Start Guide is an excellent resource for players new to the game or looking to jump right into the action. It provides a comprehensive overview of the game's mechanics, character creation, and the world in which the game takes place.

Our Quick Start Guide is still in production, but we're excited to share some of the details with you. It will be available soon [via our Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/d33kode), so stay tuned! In the video, we go over what we have so far — we wanted to focus on making it user-friendly for new players.

One of the things we've focused on in the guide is making it approachable: the writing is clear and concise, and the layout is designed to be easy to follow.

We're excited to see new players diving into the world of Shadows with the help of our Quick Start Guide. It's an excellent introduction to the game and provides everything players need to get started.

If you're looking to explore the world of Shadows RPG, check out the video and keep an eye out for our Quick Start Guide, available soon via our Patreon!

— GD Crew
