**What if the first thing we hand you isn't the whole game?** At our October 8 meeting we argued about how much of Shadows belongs in a short guide, and what happens to the person who loves it and wants more on Monday.

**Our monsters got a new building system.** Seven families of supernatural, three ways a creature can relate to being human, and a list of threats you can mix and match instead of rebuilding from scratch. One of them is a spirit that only wants to be remembered, and you can't shoot it.

**And a question about vampires turned into lore.** Whether killing a sire kills its children sounds like a rules question. It wasn't.

Plus: a cleanup so thorough it got called "patch notes," and the first look at the GM tools.
