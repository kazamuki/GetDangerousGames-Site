Hey there, Dangerinos (we really need a name for our fans)! We've got some exciting news to share with you. We recently had a blast hanging out with Nick from [20MinuteGamer](https://www.youtube.com/@20MG). It was like a virtual gaming session with an old friend — talking about games, movies, TV, our roots, and all things fun. Seriously, we recorded for an hour and then kept talking long into the night after the recording was done.

So grab your favorite snack, kick back, and let's Get Dangerous!

## Kicking It With 20MinuteGamer

First things first — if you haven't checked out Nick's channel yet, you're seriously missing out. Head over to [20MinuteGamer](https://www.youtube.com/@20MG) right now. He's got epic gameplay videos on games like Phantom Brigade, Aliens: Dark Descent, and a bunch more. His approach to the channel is unique, and he's focused on getting the best gaming experience for your time.

## Nostalgia Trip Down Gaming Lane

Our chat with Nick took us on a nostalgic trip, and we found we had a mutual love for RTS and squad-based games. It's awesome how these genres bring out the strategist and commander in all of us — you can still see it in the games we gravitate toward today. We reminisced about the gaming histories that got us hooked as kids, like one of us (that's me!) who had a Commodore 64 and an Amiga as gaming companions while everyone else was booting up DOS and Windows 3.1.

## Our Gaming Faves

In addition to talking about our roots, we discussed what's got our interest in the present, both games and publishers. We talked about the ever-changing landscape of games, the value of high-quality smaller publishers, and some AAA gems we've found along the way. There was even some nerding out over Destiny 2 and Darktide, so if you like those titles, you're in for a treat.

## Our Approach to Gaming and Content Creation

When it comes to making content, we keep it real. Our passion for gaming is what drives us, and we want to share that with you. Our true passion project is [Shadows, our original TTRPG](https://www.shadowsrpg.com) (catch us every Thursday at 8pm on [Twitch](http://twitch.tv/d33kode)). Alongside that, we're always livestreaming games — mixing newer content like Baldur's Gate 3 with older stuff like Civilization 6, some really old stuff (Shadow Warrior, anyone?), and our latest love, Elden Ring (shoutout to Nick for the gift!).

The goal of our channel is to engage the [Get Dangerous community](https://discord.com/invite/getdangerous) and give you something entertaining to sink into at the end of the day. We want you to feel safe taking a risk and trying something new, and we'll keep checking out new titles and publishers to let you know when it's time to really Get Dangerous.

## In Conclusion

For us, gaming is all about community and connecting with fellow gamers. Our chat with 20MinuteGamer was a blast, and we want you to be part of the fun too.

Check out our channel, [Get Dangerous](https://www.youtube.com/@d33kode), and let's geek out together over the world of gaming. And of course, don't forget to swing by 20MinuteGamer's channel for more epic gaming adventures. Nick, thanks a bunch for the awesome chat and for being part of our gaming journey. We're stoked to keep exploring the gaming universe with gamers like you. Stay tuned for more writing, gaming, and badassery from Get Dangerous!
