**Our October 15 design meeting started with a werewolf and ended with a corpse that wouldn't stay down.** In between, we made a few calls that had nothing to do with balance.

**We cut a power, and not for game reasons.** One piece of werewolf lore turned out to be something we had no business playing with. We found out why, and we changed it.

**Someone asked, "Can I insert an analogy?"** What followed made three bloodlines feel like three generations arguing across the same dinner table, and nobody at the meeting could un-see it.

**A question about circuit breakers produced a monster we hadn't planned on.** It started as a rules question about power and ended with a very bad night for a very good implant.

Plus: a character class that finally has a reason to exist, a December playtest on the calendar, and a very long reading list for the team.
