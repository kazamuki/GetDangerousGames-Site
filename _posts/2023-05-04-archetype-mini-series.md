Hey everyone, Melf here!

If you've been following along with our podcast [Fiction Factory](https://open.spotify.com/show/67HMykvw9NaCtPnF0LOj1k), you'll note that the last few episodes have been a little different. Rather than sticking to the world-building side of things, I recently started a deep dive into our different character archetypes and how they fit into the world of Shadows and NYTE City.

In this mini-series, we'll be delving deep into the gritty world of NYTE City and learning more about the people within. Each episode will focus on a different character archetype, exploring their skills, powers, and traits, as well as how they've managed to survive in the back alleys and boardrooms of NYTE City.

With each episode, I'll provide insight into how these archetypes are the backbone of a character's concept, and how they can be adapted to fit different storylines and campaigns.

We'll also be discussing the importance of working with your Game Master (GM) to make sure your character's archetype fits within the Power Level of the campaign, and the different ways you can use Skills, Advantages, and Freebie Points to enhance your character's capabilities.

In addition to providing valuable information for players of Shadows, the mini-series will also be a great resource for anyone interested in exploring the cyberpunk genre. And with each episode only 20 minutes long, it's the perfect way to get your feet wet without committing to a full-length podcast.

So whether you've been a longtime follower of Shadows or you're just starting out, join us for the character archetype mini-series! The first two episodes, focusing on the [versatile Human](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1wrVofrYwUVVU9IrFovC7l) and the [mystical Arcanist](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3dTXdfBNSolpmsTBkiJUkU) archetypes, are available now!
