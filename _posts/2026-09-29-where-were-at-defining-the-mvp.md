We're back at it. This stretch we defined what "finished" actually means for the Core Rulebook — five playable archetypes (Professional, Cyborg, Arcanist, Werewolf, Vampire) and a real GM guide.

Good news on progress: Arcanist and Vampire are nearly done, Professional isn't far behind, and Cyborg is closing the gap. Werewolf has the most work left, but we landed on some ideas for it we're excited about.

More on all of that — including the actual werewolf ideas — over on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/d33kode/posts/sit-rep-what-170112687).
